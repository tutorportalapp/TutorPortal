TutorPortal Pro: billing, cancellation and refunds

Last updated 20 November 2026

Plans and prices

TutorPortal Pro costs $5.99 per month or $59.99 per year. All prices are in Australian dollars. Payments are processed by Stripe, and we never see or store your card details.

Free trial

New Pro subscribers get a 7-day free trial. A card is needed to start it. If you cancel before the trial ends you will not be charged. When the trial ends, your first payment is taken and your plan renews automatically. The trial is for first-time subscribers only.

Renewal

Your plan renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period, at the price you signed up to, until you cancel.

Cancelling a monthly plan

You can cancel at any time using Manage subscription on the Pricing page. There is no termination fee. Pro stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, then your account returns to the free plan.

Cancelling a yearly plan

You can stop a yearly plan from renewing at any time using Manage subscription. There is no fee for that, and Pro stays active until the end of the year you have paid for.

If you ask us to end a yearly plan before the end of the paid year, an early termination fee applies. The fee is 50% of the value of the full unused months remaining. Each month is valued at $5.00 (the yearly price divided by 12, rounded to the nearest cent). Only full unused months count: a part-used month is not counted. We end your plan and refund the unused amount after the fee has been taken out.

Example: you are 5.5 months into a yearly plan. There are 6 full unused months, worth $30.00. The fee is 50%, which is $15.00, so $15.00 is refunded and Pro ends. To request this, use the contact form on the home page from the email address on your account.

Refunds

Except for the early termination of a yearly plan described above, payments are not refundable. This includes unused time, part periods, and not cancelling before a renewal date. You can avoid being charged for the next period by cancelling before the renewal date shown on the Pricing page.

After you cancel

Your saved papers and starred questions are kept, but locked, until you resubscribe. Free plan features carry on as normal.

Your rights under Australian law

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If there is a major problem with the service, or we make a billing mistake, you may be entitled to a remedy, including a refund.

Changes

If the price of Pro changes, we will tell you before the new price applies to your next renewal.

Questions

Use the contact form on the home page.
