How it works

1

Choose schools, topics and difficulty

Then set the size by number of questions, total marks or working time. Section I is multiple choice and Section II is written response.

2

Preview and print

Parts of the same question stay together, questions run easy to hard, and diagrams come with them. Papers print on A4 with a cover page showing total marks and working time.

3

Print a student copy and a tutor copy

Print the paper on its own for students, and switch to the answer key for a tutor copy. The key gives answers only, not worked solutions.

4

Keep your history

With a profile, every paper is saved so you can re-print it or avoid repeating questions.

Browse before you build

The Library lists papers by school or year, and questions by topic. Star the questions you want to come back to, then filter to starred only.
