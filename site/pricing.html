Questions

How does the free trial work?
You get 7 days of Pro with no charge. You enter a card to start, and your first payment is taken when the trial ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. The trial is for first-time subscribers.
How much does it cost?
$5.99 AUD a month, or $59.99 AUD a year, which is about two months free.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Use Manage subscription on this page to stop renewal or update your card. Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Cancelling a monthly plan has no fee. Ending a yearly plan early carries a fee of 50% of the full unused months, as set out in the subscription terms.
Do you give refunds?
No. Payments are not refundable, except for the early termination of a yearly plan (where the unused amount is refunded after the fee) and where Australian Consumer Law gives you a remedy. If you cancel during the free trial you are not charged. See the subscription terms.
What happens to my saved papers and starred questions if I cancel?
They are kept, but locked. They come back as soon as you resubscribe.
Who handles payments?
Stripe. TutorPortal never sees or stores your card details.
I was charged and something is wrong.
Use the contact form on the home page and we will sort it out.
